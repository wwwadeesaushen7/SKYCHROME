# Included third-party components

- Three.js r180: exact core/renderer modules from the supplied v2.7.0 release.
  MIT notice: `public/licenses/three.txt`.
- Supabase JavaScript SDK and bundled components: exact account module from the
  supplied v2.7.0 release. MIT notice: `public/licenses/supabase.txt`.
- Manrope and Space Grotesk fonts: SIL Open Font License notices under
  `public/licenses/`.
- Build/test dependencies are pinned in `package-lock.json` and carry their own
  license files in installed npm packages.

The aircraft, spacecraft, cockpit, terrain geometry, trees and orbital station
are generated by this project's source. No external aircraft models or film
audio were downloaded. X-wing and TIE fighter names/design references appear
in the requested Star Wars fan mode; this project is not an official game.

The planet and nebula textures, procedural fallback terrain textures and
rotary-cannon audio are original procedural work. The cannon audio is
synthesized, not a recording of a real weapon.

Image files are bundled unchanged. Materials apply color tints, triplanar
projection and blending; the dawn lighting capture is white-balanced at runtime.
Exact upstream URLs, licenses, sizes and SHA-256 hashes are in
`public/textures/manifest.json`. Player-facing attribution is available from
Guide → Visual credits, at `public/credits.html` (or `credits.html` in the
compiled website). License notices are under `licenses/` in the website.
