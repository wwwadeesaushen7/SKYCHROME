Visual credits

SKYCHROME v3.0.0 combines original procedural aircraft and scenery with the following photographic materials and lighting captures.

Rock, grass and sand

Rocky Ground base color, normal and metallic/roughness maps; grass.jpg; and sand.jpg by the Babylon.js Assets contributors. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The images are bundled unchanged; the game applies surface projection, color tints and material blending.

Bundled license text · Original assets

Ice

Ice 002 by ambientCG, under CC0 1.0. Color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps are distributed through the Three.js examples.

HDR lighting

Kiara 1 Dawn and Empty Warehouse 01 from HDRI Haven / Poly Haven, under CC0, distributed through drei-assets. The outdoor capture receives a daylight white balance in the renderer.

Original work and libraries

Aircraft and spacecraft geometry, cockpit instruments, terrain shapes, trees, orbital station, planet textures, cloud shaders and rotary-cannon synthesis are generated by SKYCHROME's source. X-wing and TIE fighter references appear in the requested Star Wars fan mode; this is an unofficial project.
